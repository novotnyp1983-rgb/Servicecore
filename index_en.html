My Story

A Specialist You Can Rely On

I have over 20 years of professional experience in assembly, service, and maintenance planning of complex industrial technologies and automated warehouse systems.

For the past 11 years (since 2015), I worked directly in Germany for the renowned manufacturer Maschinenfabrik Herbert MEYER GmbH. I started as an electromechanician building control panels and installing electrical components, and subsequently advanced to the position of Senior Service Specialist. My responsibilities included installation, commissioning, testing, calibration, troubleshooting, and customer operator training for multinational companies worldwide.

Prior to that, I spent 8 years (2004–2012) at KASYS s.r.o. as a service specialist for Hänel automated warehouse systems, and from 2007 onwards as the Head of the Service Department, managing installation teams, planning services, and communicating directly with the German manufacturer.

As an independent freelancer (OSVČ), I bring you top-tier factory know-how, direct communication, flexible response times, and highly competitive prices.

What We Do

Comprehensive Service Offerings

Utilizing deep structural and process knowledge to maintain the optimal performance of your manufacturing lines.

Mechanical & Electrical Repair

Troubleshooting faults, resolving electrical issues on drives, sensors, and heaters. Replacement of bearings, shafts, chain gears, fans, and worn components.

Conveyor Belt Replacement

Expert installation, tracking, and tensioning of upper and lower PTFE, silicone, or textile conveyor belts to prevent premature belt wear and alignment faults.

Calibration & Adjustments

Alignment of pressure rollers and heating plates. Parallelism tests using pressure-sensitive film. High-precision setting of the gap clearance for uniform bonding.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled audits to identify wear before breakdown occurs. Cleaning of heating/cooling elements (using Press Cleaning Boy), lubrication, and safety checks.

Upgrades & Retrofits

Modifying machinery for new materials, installing additional sensors, upgrading heating control zones, or integrating unwinding/rewinding units.

Training & Consulting

Consultations on parameters for various bonding/laminating processes. Operator and maintenance staff training to optimize workflow and prevent failures.

Expertise

Machinery We Service

While specializing in the portfolio of Maschinenfabrik Herbert MEYER GmbH, I also service similar machinery from other manufacturers.

Double Belt Flatbed Laminators

Continuous laminating and pressing of thermoplastic composites, foams, membranes, and technical textiles. Typical models: MEYER APV, KRF, and RPS series.

Fusing Presses

Machinery for fusing interlinings in the apparel and textile industry. Serving models like MEYER AHV, RPS-Mini, and similar configurations.

Coating & Hotmelt Systems

Systems for powder scattering, paste application, or gravure roller hotmelt coating. Maintenance of application heads and gap calibrations.
